Understanding Cancer, Degenerative Disease & Psychoneuroimmunology

🧬 Cancer

What is Cancer?

Cancer is a disease where abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and can spread to other parts of the body.

Risk Factors

Genetics, lifestyle, environment, chronic inflammation.

Prevention

Healthy lifestyle, screening, vaccination, early detection.

🌿 Degenerative Disease

Definition

Degenerative diseases involve progressive damage or loss of function of tissues or organs over time.

Examples

Neurodegenerative disease, metabolic degeneration, aging-related cellular damage.

Key Concept

Oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, chronic inflammation.

🧠 Psychoneuroimmunology

Definition

Study of interaction between psychological processes, nervous system, and immune system.

Why Important

Stress, emotional state, and mental health can influence immune response and disease progression.

Application

Stress management, holistic therapy, mind-body medicine.
